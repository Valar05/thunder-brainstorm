# Game-Idea Abundance and the Audition Rule

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** active brainstorm / selection doctrine

## Observation

Looking back through old games reliably creates a surge of new game-making desire. The recurring problem is not lack of ideas. It is **idea abundance**: many ideas become simultaneously attractive, and choosing one becomes harder than generating another.

This is consistent with Drew's broader creative pattern: hypothesis generation is cheap and continuous. The bottleneck is candidate selection.

## Selection error to avoid

Do not ask:

```text
Which idea deserves to become my next game?
```

That makes the choice too expensive too early.

Instead ask:

```text
Which idea deserves the cheapest useful audition next?
```

An audition is not a commitment to a project. It is the smallest playable or mechanically revealing proof that can generate honest desire and feedback.

## Audition loop

```text
IDEA ABUNDANCE
-> pick one cheap discriminating question
-> build the smallest proof that answers it
-> play immediately
-> observe whether curiosity increases or collapses
-> preserve the useful result
-> either continue or return to the pool
```

The winner is not necessarily the idea with the strongest pitch. It is the one that becomes more interesting after contact with reality.

## Suggested gates

A candidate advances when the proof produces at least one of these:

- an interaction that is fun before content exists;
- a mechanic that invites immediate variation;
- a surprising failure that generates a better question;
- a body/control/animation relationship that feels worth inhabiting;
- a result Drew wants to touch again after the initial novelty passes.

A candidate can be shelved without being declared bad. The pool is an archive, not a graveyard.

## Compact doctrine

> **Do not choose the next game. Choose the next audition.**

> **Idea abundance is not indecision upstream. It is a selection problem downstream.**

The objective is to keep commitment cheap enough that several ideas can touch reality before one earns the right to become expensive.
