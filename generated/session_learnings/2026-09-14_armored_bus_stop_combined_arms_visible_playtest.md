# Armored Bus Stop — Combined Arms Became Visible

Live phone playtest confirmation from Drew:

> "combined arms is actually visible and pretty cool"

This matters because the desired effect was not merely that suppression, maneuver, vehicle shock, moving cover, crossfire, and target prioritization exist in code. The player could perceive their interaction directly in play.

## Validation

A combined-arms model has crossed an important threshold when the player can *see the relationship between capabilities* without needing a doctrine label or explanatory HUD.

The useful test is therefore not:

> Are multiple arms implemented?

It is:

> Can the player see one capability making another capability more effective?

In Armored Bus Stop, the early playtest signal says yes.

## Design consequence

Preserve legibility while increasing sophistication. Do not bury the visible causal chain under more systems.

Current visible chain:

CARRIER DELIVERY -> SUPPRESSION -> MANEUVER -> VEHICLE SHOCK / MOVING COVER -> CROSSFIRE -> CLEAR

## Rule

**Combined arms is not a list of units. It is visible cooperation between unlike capabilities.**
