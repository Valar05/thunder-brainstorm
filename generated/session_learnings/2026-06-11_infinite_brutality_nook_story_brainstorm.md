# Infinite Brutality Nook Story Brainstorm

Date: 2026-06-11

## Project

Infinite Brutality's small environmental nooks and dioramas created a major tonal shift: the world stopped reading as abstract combat geometry and began to feel lived in. The next design step is to let those nooks carry fragmented absent-people story.

This phase assumes:

- no visible living population yet
- only evidence that people were here
- broad corpus intake rather than one prestige-text source
- environmental writing as forensic worldbuilding, not codex-lore drops

## Core Shift

The right unit of story is not a room monologue or lore plaque. It is a micro-history packet attached to a nook.

Each nook packet should encode:

- what the place originally was
- how later survivors repurposed it
- what interrupted or damaged that use
- what fragment of the wider run-story it reveals

This turns shelter corners, repair alcoves, shrine shelves, ration stations, and burial recesses into story-bearing spaces.

## Broad Corpus Rule

The corpus should be broad, real, and unabridged. The target is not "Homer but darker." It is a layered field of voices:

- epic and tragedy for public memory and civilizational scale
- scripture and lament for ritual and grief language
- letters, rules, and ledgers for daily life
- siege, plague, exile, and reconstruction writing for collapse pressure
- child/domestic materials for tenderness and repetition
- project-local canon for recurrence and anti-generic specificity

General lesson: broad corpus intake should pressure diction, motif, and social imagination, not produce direct pasted allusion.

## Best Procedural Unit

The strongest generation unit is a `nook story packet`, not a whole-room prose slab.

Useful packet fields:

- `former_use`
- `survival_use`
- `speaker_class`
- `dominant_verb`
- `survival_pressure`
- `taboo_or_rule`
- `evidence_objects`
- `text_fragments`
- `symbol_fragments`
- `absence_or_interruption`
- `story_function`

This keeps procedural writing spatial and inspectable.

## Story Axes Per Run

A run should silently choose:

- origin population
- rupture event
- survival doctrine
- taboo
- hope

All nook packets then refract those same axes through different local uses. This creates fragmented coherence without linear exposition.

## First Five Survivor Cultures

The first pass should not invent dozens of factions. It should start with five overlapping survivor cultures:

- Terrace Households
- Rope-Market Carriers
- Chapel Custodians
- Furnace Tenders
- Toll Remnant Scribes

These are social formations revealed by traces, not NPC quest-giver factions.
