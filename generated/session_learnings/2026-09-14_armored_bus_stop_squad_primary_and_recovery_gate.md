# Armored Bus Stop — Squad Primary / Recovery Gate

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** active playtest doctrine

## Correction

The first APC prototype made the carrier mechanically interesting while treating infantry as mostly static deployed guns. That misses the strongest Last Convoy relationship.

The carrier should be powerful, but the squad should be the most powerful part of the combined system.

```text
APC = mobility + protection + delivery + extraction + ramming
INFANTRY = battlefield power
```

The squad therefore should not merely occupy three cover points and wait. After deployment it should establish, acquire threats, spread, advance to useful range, suppress, focus fire, and clear the local fight autonomously.

## Recovery is a separate maneuver

Drew's important constraint:

> Infantry cannot get back in while the carrier is still driving.

That converts extraction into another consequence of the same movement grammar without adding an action button.

```text
slow / stop at bus stop -> squad dismounts
squad fights -> stop becomes secure
survivors return toward rear hatch
moving carrier -> no boarding
stopped carrier + rear-hatch proximity -> survivors board automatically
```

The squad can fight while moving back toward the carrier, but reboarding requires the APC to be genuinely near rest. This makes stopping useful twice: first for deployment, later for custody and recovery.

## Last Convoy continuity

The lesson is not merely "have followers." It is that the player's vehicle becomes more capable because allied capability exists outside the player body.

```text
player vehicle = carrier / geometry / risk manager
allied unit = distributed offensive capability
```

The player should feel capable alone and dramatically more capable when the squad is alive, positioned, and recoverable.

## Compact rules

> **The carrier delivers power. The squad is the power.**

> **A moving vehicle may carry momentum, but it may not magically carry people who have not climbed back in.**

> **Deployment and extraction should both emerge from stopping, not from adding buttons.**
