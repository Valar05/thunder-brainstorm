# Infinite Brutality Hanging Gardens Story Pilot

Date: 2026-06-11

## Project

The general absent-people environmental writing system has now been collapsed into one concrete Hanging Gardens district pilot.

The important move was to stop at a district slice rather than trying to invent the whole setting at once.

## What The Pilot Adds

- one hidden run-story state
- one district role
- 12 concrete nook packets
- repeated motifs for coherence
- a narrow source packet for future writing-agent deepening

## Chosen Story Pressure

This pilot is domestic and infrastructural, not aristocratic or prophetic.

Core state:

- Terrace Households under pressure
- Rope-Market Carrier route knowledge
- aqueduct poisoning rupture
- concealment and rationed speech doctrine
- dusk naming taboo
- eastward escape hope

This creates the right kind of human residue for a floating-garden settlement: bowls, route knots, veiled names, child copy lines, reserve jars, broken customs law.

## Why It Works

The best detail is not that the world has lore. The best detail is that the player can infer habits:

- how children were taught
- how rations were counted
- how names were hidden
- how escape was prepared
- how former garden craft became body and rope care

That is what makes a place feel lived in.

## Reusable Pattern

A strong next-step pattern for environmental story generation is:

1. broad corpus bands
2. hidden run-story axes
3. one district role
4. 8 to 12 nook packets
5. repeated motifs
6. one narrow source packet for text refinement

This is much safer and more inspectable than asking for full-setting lore generation in one pass.
