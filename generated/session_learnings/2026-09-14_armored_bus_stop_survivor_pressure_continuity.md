# Armored Bus Stop — Survivor Pressure Continuity

## Playtest correction

"Infinite" does not mean discrete recycled encounters separated by travel air. It means the run never resets between fights. There are no wave rooms and no authored downtime gates. Combat pressure is continuous, while local density breathes up and down.

## Rule

**TIME IS PROGRESSION; PRESSURE IS CONTINUOUS; DENSITY BREATHES.**

Bus stops are tactical opportunities inside the pressure field, not rooms that switch combat on.

### Director behavior
- Ambient enemies spawn around the APC whether or not a bus stop is active.
- Enemy target count varies continuously so there are crowded moments and lighter moments without intentional emptiness.
- Time still raises the baseline and introduces more heavies / specialists.
- Active bus-stop fights bias some reinforcements onto the objective, producing spikes without pausing the surrounding war.
- Ambient contacts actively pursue the APC or squad instead of waiting near a dormant objective.

## Design distinction

Incorrect: CLEAR ROOM → TRAVEL AIR → NEW ROOM → CLEAR.

Correct: CONTINUOUS CONTACT FIELD + TEMPORARY DENSITY WAVES + BUS-STOP TACTICAL SPIKES.

The map is stage machinery. The pressure clock is the run.
