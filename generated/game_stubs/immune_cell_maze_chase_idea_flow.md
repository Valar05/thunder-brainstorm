# Immune Cell Maze-Chase Idea Flow

## Why This Theme Is Stronger

The arcade logic now has natural verbs: tag tissue, engulf infection, learn antigens, survive fever, and route through damaged membranes. Antibodies are not arbitrary pellets; they are the floor objective. Infection agents are not just chasers; each one can represent a different way disease learns the body.

## The Key Design Move

Do not make adaptations simple powerups. Treat them like fleshpunk body choices: each route advantage creates a readable weakness or future recognition problem.

## Strong First Playable

- One 21x21 tissue maze.
- 80 antibodies.
- 4 complement bursts.
- 4 infection agents.
- Fever meter.
- One memory antigen.
- Three body adaptations after clearing the floor.
- Debug overlay with infection mode and target tile.
