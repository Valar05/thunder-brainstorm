# Through the Slit — Twin-Tread Endless Breach — Codex Synthesis

## Verdict

Build **The Acre Must Live**.

Borrow two pieces from the other proposals:

- From **The Grinding Line**: bounded sector cards may overlap at the slit so the next threat is already visible before the current acre is fully safe.
- From **The Machine Remembers**: every graft occupies a physical body socket with a visible arc, and wounds remain directional.

Do not borrow The Grinding Line's uninterrupted endless-road structure or The Machine Remembers' recipes, adaptive anatomy, and delayed-consequence campaign yet. Both are expensive ways to avoid proving the central pleasure.

The central pleasure is:

> Two treads shape one moving shield, one formation, and several firing nerves. The machine opens the wound; living infantry make the wound belong to you.

## Hunger Finding

Removing manual fire did not remove authorship. It moved authorship into:

- hull facing;
- distance;
- protected formation geometry;
- weapon arcs;
- recoil;
- breach width;
- build choice.

v22 failed because those systems ended before they accumulated. A survivor-like needs repeated pressure, guaranteed build mutation, and an increasingly legible body. Thirty seconds with no offer is an interaction sketch, not a run.

## Exact Battle Contract

During active battle, telemetry may record only:

- `left_tread` pressure;
- `right_tread` pressure.

During a frozen graft gate, telemetry may additionally record:

- `upgrade_card_1`;
- `upgrade_card_2`;
- `upgrade_card_3`.

No other player action is legal.

Organs acquire only through their world-space arcs, obey terrain occlusion, spawn physical projectiles, and keep independent reload rhythms. Steering chooses violence by changing those conditions.

## Authored Opening: 0–90 Seconds

### 0–8: Wake behind the slit

- The living hull wakes in first person.
- Only two thumb tread rails appear.
- Infantry mass in the hull's protected lee.
- Holding both treads advances. Differential pressure yaws.
- No weapon fires until the forward nerve crosses a visible enemy.

### 8–22: MG lesson

- A broad infantry screen and one MG team occupy the trench lip.
- MG rounds strike and kill exposed infantry; they cannot damage hull tissue.
- Steering the forward nerve across the MG team lets the main maw fire.
- Turning too far exposes the formation, proving that firing geometry and protection geometry are related but not identical.

### 22–36: Width lesson

- A wire belt presents:
  - one broad gap that passes the formation;
  - one narrow shortcut that places a tempting gun crew in the maw's arc.
- The hull can pass the shortcut; the war party cannot.
- HE impact physically clears wire. Capture width is measured in world geometry, not a scripted flag.

### 36–48: Directional heavy threat

- A port AP gun begins a readable traverse and commits.
- The player yaws the maw onto its crew while presenting port living scutes.
- The penetrator is a physical projectile.
- A hit outside the scute angle damages a named port organ; a hit inside the coverage is deflected and consumes scute integrity.

### 48–55: First acre

- Surviving infantry cross the cleared width and occupy the trench.
- The acre pays out shells, tissue, and replacements.
- An offer token minted at crossing resolves now.
- The game freezes for the first three-card graft choice no later than 55 seconds.

### 55–67: Body mutation

- The selected graft appears on the living hull.
- Its firing nerve or protection geometry is projected for two seconds.
- Existing organs remain visible and active.
- Concrete current-to-next deltas appear on the card and in the brief resume banner.

### 67–90: Reprisal

- A counterattack combines:
  - mass infantry;
  - one carrier that breaks into a child assault threat;
  - one observer painting an artillery lane;
  - one satchel pair with commitment and rearm behavior.
- Every opening graft has an authored immediate use:
  - Bow Gunner catches the infantry shoulder.
  - Top Gunner can remove the observer or carrier child.
  - Living Scutes change the safe answer to the AP follow-up.
- At 86 seconds the next slit begins opening.
- At 90 seconds the next breach is already live.
- There is no victory screen.

## Endless Macro-Cycle

```text
BREACH 35–50s
  Open formation-width route; defeat or turn heavy threats
CROSS 10–15s
  Keep surviving infantry inside the moving lee
CONSOLIDATE 15–25s
  Hold the acre against counterfire; mint supplies and offer token
GRAFT 4–6s frozen
  Choose one of three additive body mutations
REPRISAL 25–40s
  Immediate proof of the new graft under a mixed counterattack
NEXT SLIT
  Overlap the next bounded sector card; increase depth; repeat
```

The director owns pacing, not victory. It draws bounded cards for geometry, enemy branch, complication, and payout modifier. A run ends only when the directional hull fails or the war party drops below capture strength.

## Initial Graft Deck

### Always eligible at Acre 1

1. **Bow Gunner**
   - Adds a 32-degree forward anti-infantry arc.
   - Independent cadence: 1.0 shot/s.
   - Level II adds a second heavy pulse at 0.5 shot/s; it does not replace Level I.

2. **Top Gunner**
   - Adds a 110-degree high arc.
   - Targets observers and carrier threats only.
   - Independent cadence: 0.7 shot/s.
   - Cannot target ground AP pits.

3. **Living Scutes**
   - Adds 18 degrees of front-quarter AP coverage on both sides.
   - Each side deflects one penetrator before a 7s regrowth.
   - Level II allows two deflections inside the same geometry.

### Eligible after one weapon organ

4. **Recoil Tendons**
   - Main-maw recoil becomes a short reverse shove.
   - Pulls the protected lee backward 8 units.
   - Creates a timing tool; it does not increase damage.

5. **Counterbattery Bladder**
   - Surviving 1.5s of observer paint stores the observer's last visible position.
   - Fires one physical lofted shell.
   - Independent cooldown: 8s.

6. **Barbed Tongue**
   - Main-maw HE impacts pull loose wire inward by 12 units.
   - Widens formation routes.
   - Does not increase armor damage.

### Eligible after first satchel attack

7. **Satchel Cilia**
   - Adds a 24-degree close front skirt.
   - Knocks one committed satchel attacker away.
   - Regrowth: 5s.
   - No effect on bullets, MG, AP, or artillery.

### Eligible after a clean capture

8. **Infantry Cloister**
   - Widens the protected lee from 34 to 46 world units.
   - Adds one infantry file to maximum formation width.
   - Gives no hull protection.

9. **Brood Chapel**
   - Clean capture stores three infantry replacements instead of one.
   - Replacements deploy only during the next crossing.
   - Storage appears physically on the hull.

## Offer Rules

- Offer exactly three cards.
- At least one card must alter firing or protection geometry.
- At least one card must change infantry, recoil, or terrain interaction.
- Never offer a capped card.
- Never offer a dependent card before its prerequisite event.
- Duplicate levels show concrete current → next values.
- A card cannot deactivate or replace an earned organ.
- The first offer is the fixed Bow Gunner / Top Gunner / Living Scutes trio so the immediate reprisal can be authored against all three.
- Later offers are seeded but constrained by the current body, wounds, enemy deck, and missing role coverage.

## Director Escalation

Escalate decisions, not only health.

1. **Population**
   - More infantry bodies and denser MG lanes.

2. **Geometry**
   - Offset wire gaps, angled traverses, crater occlusion, and narrower formation choices.

3. **Crossfire**
   - AP commitment from different quarters, never untelegraphed simultaneous hits.

4. **Observation**
   - More observers, shorter paint windows, overlapping but escapable artillery lanes.

5. **Commitment threats**
   - Satchel route count, carrier branches, and explicit rearm timing.

6. **Scarcity**
   - Payout modifiers force tradeoffs among shells, tissue, and replacements.

7. **Overlap**
   - The next slit becomes visible earlier, increasing planning pressure without erasing consolidation.

8. **Adaptation**
   - After five minutes, enemy deck weights respond to the player's strongest arc, but do not gain immunity or invalidate the build.

## Required Telemetry

Each run records:

- active time;
- macro phase;
- acre/sector depth;
- left and right tread pressure;
- hull yaw and speed;
- protected-lee occupancy;
- formation survivors;
- cleared breach width;
- offer token minted/resolved;
- offered and selected grafts;
- organ, socket, valid arc, occlusion result, target class, projectile spawn, and hit;
- heavy-threat warning, commitment, impact, and rearm;
- damage recipient and damage class;
- loss cause.

A deterministic same-seed replay harness must compare two materially different tread traces. If kills, capture timing, formation survival, and threat interruption remain substantially identical, movement is still decorative and the build fails.

## Acceptance Gates Before Real Graphics

- Ten-minute automated smoke run:
  - no terminal victory;
  - at least eight acre transitions;
  - at least eight resolved offer tokens;
  - bounded entity counts;
  - no stuck macro phase.

- Five-minute human phone run:
  - first graft by 60s;
  - at least five total graft choices;
  - at least three moments where the player intentionally changes hull yaw to acquire or deny an arc;
  - first graft has a visible consequence in the immediate reprisal;
  - both treads remain meaningful during breach, cross, consolidation, and reprisal.

- Input audit:
  - only tread pressure during battle;
  - only upgrade-card choice during graft freeze;
  - no hidden aim, fire, ammo, ability, or designation input.

- Damage audit:
  - MG → infantry only;
  - AP/artillery/satchel → hull only after warning/commitment;
  - no ambient hull drain;
  - directional scutes work only inside declared geometry.

- Progression audit:
  - every captured acre resolves exactly one offer token;
  - no silent replacement;
  - capped and prerequisite cards obey eligibility;
  - six-graft bodies remain visually and mechanically legible.

- Causality audit:
  - every shot had a valid arc and physical path;
  - different tread traces on the same seed produce materially different combat and capture results.

## Why This Is Not v20

v20 gave automatic violence no consequential locomotion. This design makes distance, yaw, occlusion, recoil, armor facing, formation shelter, and breach width all consequences of the two tread inputs.

## Why This Is Not v21

v21 restored consequence by adding manual gunnery. This design has no firing, aiming, designation, ammunition, or ability verbs. The hull's geometry is the target selector.

## Why This Is Not v22

v22 ended before a survivor-like run existed. This design guarantees the first graft by 55–60 seconds, repeats grafts roughly every fifty seconds, overlaps the next slit before the current cycle ends, and has explicit five- and ten-minute gates with no victory state.
