# default-simulation-corpus-gate Skill

Status: ACTIVE / GLOBAL DEFAULT
Authority: Drew correction, 2026-09-07

## Purpose

Every substantive commission must enter through a simulation corpus before the operator produces the answer, artifact, plan, critique, or implementation. This is a default operating gate, not an optional style pass.

The user does not have to say `Tango`, `Thunder Brainstorm`, `Story Brainstorm`, `Fortress Online`, or any other incantation to activate it.

## Non-optional law

**No corpus, no composition.**

Before responding to a commission:

1. Identify the task class.
2. Select at least one appropriate simulation corpus.
3. Retrieve actual corpus evidence, patterns, source cards, prior scenes, mechanics, or indexed references.
4. Let the corpus change the working model.
5. Only then synthesize the response or execute the task.

Merely naming a corpus does not satisfy the gate. The output must show evidence of transfer: a changed mechanism, altered structure, recovered constraint, surprising comparison, rejected easy answer, or source-derived move.

## Corpus routing

### Story / prose / scene / character / anthology work

Prefer **Story Brainstorm** when available.

Story Brainstorm exists to mine rights-verified pre-1930 written works into narrative primitive cards. Use it for scene structure, pacing, dialogue mechanics, withholding, procedural danger, bodily observation, social friction, point of view, and narrative compression.

When the Story Brainstorm repo is not yet populated enough for the needed pass, route through the available rights-verified literary corpus that feeds or precedes it, including Vernepunk source material, while preserving Story Brainstorm's evidence discipline.

If Jules Verne and H. G. Wells are designated donor authors for the current fiction lane, their use is mandatory unless a more specific user instruction overrides it. Do not merely cite their names. Mine actual source behavior or established corpus organs from each author.

Do not imitate sentences. Extract mechanisms.

Examples of valid transfer:
- Verne: operational dialogue; machinery or procedure carrying suspense; danger described through concrete process; consequence moving through systems.
- Wells: social normality colliding with the impossible; embodied awkwardness; observational compression; ontological incompatibility left active instead of explained away.

### Cross-domain invention / systems / mechanics / product or game synthesis

Prefer **Thunder Brainstorm**.

Use its indexed mechanic corpus, source packets, session learnings, pattern cards, and project references to retrieve structurally similar systems before inventing from scratch.

### Mixed commissions

Use more than one corpus only when each adds a materially different axis. Do not concatenate corpora ceremonially.

Example: a story scene with a mechanical pressure system may use Story Brainstorm for narrative behavior and Thunder Brainstorm for state/consequence mechanics.

### Future brainstorm corpora

Any later corpus explicitly promoted by Drew enters the resolver. Choose the smallest sufficient corpus set for the commission.

## Fiction gate

For fiction generation or revision, the corpus pass must happen before prose.

Minimum preflight:
- source canon and protected unknowns;
- current scene pressure;
- at least one recovered narrative primitive / donor mechanism;
- at least one contradiction or alternate route surfaced by corpus comparison;
- Fictional Discovery Simulation actor state and judgment machinery when simulation is requested or implied;
- no moral/theme target preloaded as plot.

If the pass cannot produce at least one non-obvious structural change, the corpus use was decorative. Run another pass before writing.

## Weak-draft gate

When a draft has good bones but does not land:

1. Do not polish it.
2. Retrieve corpus analogues.
3. She-Raw finds the easiest story and the hidden seam.
4. Tetsuya removes non-load-bearing material.
5. Compare against a successful non-event / pressure artifact when applicable.
6. Re-run the scene from the last trusted state rather than line-editing the failed delivery.

## Public-domain donor discipline

When mining public-domain or rights-cleared works:
- preserve source identity and provenance;
- quote minimally; prefer paraphrased mechanism extraction;
- separate observation from inference;
- do not counterfeit authorial style;
- do not let a famous work dominate simply because it is famous;
- favor less-obvious donor moments when they solve the actual mechanism better.

## Anti-failure rules

Reject a pass if:
- the same answer would have been produced without corpus retrieval;
- the corpus is named but not consulted;
- a source is used as decorative analogy;
- prose is written first and corpus justification is added afterward;
- the operator falls back to generic assistant cadence despite available donor mechanisms;
- a designated donor author such as Verne or Wells is silently omitted;
- one neat lesson replaces a live contradiction the corpus exposed.

## Acceptance

A commission passes this gate when the operator can identify:

**Corpus used -> evidence retrieved -> mechanism transferred -> output changed.**

This chain may remain internal unless the user asks for receipts, but it must exist.

## Compact law

**The clock fires first. The corpus fires before composition. Both are default.**
