# Source Packet: Infinite Brutality Nook TTS Lines

## Goal

Write one spoken OpenAI TTS line for each Hanging Gardens nook packet.

These are not codex entries and not direct prop text. They are short third-person environmental narration lines that play when the player nears a nook.

## Project

- Project: Infinite Brutality
- Track: Hanging Gardens absent-people environmental story
- Runtime target: one triggered speech clip per packet id

## Hard Constraints

- One sentence only per packet id.
- Usually 8 to 18 words.
- No dialogue quotation marks.
- No first person.
- No visible living people in present tense.
- No named heroes.
- No codex or lore-dump voice.
- Must sound like a concise narrator observing residue, not like a prop label.
- Must encapsulate the nook's human situation, not merely repeat the shortest fragment.
- Keep the sentence playable as TTS: clear syntax, no semicolons, no list feel.

## Desired Voice

Severe, compressed, human, archaeological without academic distance.
Think one sentence that tells the player what kind of life or loss happened here.
