# Source Packet: Infinite Brutality Hanging Gardens Nook Story Pilot

## Goal

Draft stronger, more evocative short environmental text fragments for a first-person melee/platformer world where no living people are yet visible. The player should only see evidence that people were here.

Do not write codex entries or omniscient lore paragraphs. Write fragmentary environmental writing tied to specific nook packets.

## Project

- Project: Infinite Brutality
- World family: Hanging Gardens / floating gardens settlement over the abyss
- Runtime shape: phone-first Three.js first-person melee/platformer
- Current task: deepen one district's absent-people story through nook packets

## Hidden Run Story State

Use this exact state:

- origin population: Terrace Households
- secondary influence: Rope-Market Carriers
- rupture event: aqueduct poisoning
- survival doctrine: concealment and rationed speech
- taboo: do not name the dead after dusk
- hope: eastward ascent to the lit terraces

## District Role

`intake_terraces_and_bridge_customs`

This district used to sort arrivals, weigh goods, and manage water/toll flow. Survivors now hide households, reserve stores, memorials, and route signals inside that official architecture.

## Required Writing Rules

- Every fragment must sound like it belongs to a speaker class.
- Every fragment must have a practical reason to exist at that location.
- Keep most fragments under 16 words.
- Prefer rule, tally, vow, warning, memorial, or child-copy forms.
- Keep the language human, local, and pressured.
- No named heroes.
- No grand myth exposition.
- No visible living NPC presence.
- No modern phrasing.
- No lore-tablet voice.
