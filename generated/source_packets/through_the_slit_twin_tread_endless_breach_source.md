# Through the Slit — Twin-Tread Endless Breach Source Packet

Date: 2026-07-30
Status: bounded Thunder Brainstorm input
Project: Through the Slit
Authority: user commission locks and the current game bible; deployed v22 is evidence of a rejected implementation, not design authority

## Goal

Produce three distinct, evidence-backed designs for an endless mobile-first Three.js survivor-like about piloting the unseen biomechanical landship answer to Vanguard through a Great War breach.

The run must be authored entirely through:

1. independent left-tread pressure,
2. independent right-tread pressure,
3. discrete graft choices at earned upgrade gates.

There is no live battle input for firing, aiming, target designation, ammunition, abilities, or infantry orders.

The output must solve the failures proven by v22: a run that effectively ends around thirty seconds, exposes no upgrade choice, and lets automatic violence coexist with movement instead of being authored by it.

## Commission Locks

### Identity and view

- First-person Three.js Great War Fleshpunk.
- The player pilots an unseen living landship built as the biomechanical answer to Vanguard.
- The machine has twin living treads, directional living armor, physical ballistic weapon organs, additive grafts, and a dependent infantry war party.
- Enemy infantry must remain the dominant battlefield population.

### Control contract

- Battle-time continuous input is exactly two tread rails: left and right.
- Pressing both drives or brakes the hull; differential pressure yaws it.
- No fire button.
- No aim surface.
- No target designation.
- No ammunition switching.
- No ability buttons.
- Keyboard testing may mirror the same two rails but may not add verbs.
- Upgrade cards are allowed only at explicit earned gates.

### Movement-to-violence law

- Hull-fixed organs may acquire and fire only through readable physical arcs.
- Shells and projectiles are physical world objects.
- Hull position and facing select violence by bringing a threat into an organ's firing nerve.
- No omniscient 360-degree lawnmower targeting.
- Steering into a firing geometry must visibly produce a different combat outcome from steering away from it.
- Independent organs keep independent rhythms; grafts may add arcs, cadences, projectile traits, recoil behavior, or support behavior.

### Armor and enemy damage law

- Living armor is directional geometry, not a generic damage-reduction stat.
- Machine guns threaten infantry, not the landship's health bar.
- Armor-piercing guns, close satchel threats, and observer-called artillery are the primary hull threats.
- AP commitment, artillery observation, and other catastrophic attacks must be warned, physical, and interruptible by positioning.
- Contact enemies require readable approach, commitment, and rearm gaps; they may not stick to the hull and spam invisible damage.

### Breach and capture law

- The landship can open a route but cannot capture land alone.
- Infantry survival converts the opened route into occupied ground.
- Breach width must matter to the following formation.
- Captured ground must pay out ammunition, tissue repair, replacements, and/or a graft gate.
- A first graft must meet an immediate counterattack so its effect is felt, not merely listed.

### Progression law

- The run is endless until the landship or war party fails.
- "Endless" means repeated playable breach/consolidation pressure with continued upgrades and escalation, not a thirty-second script with a final victory state.
- Upgrade offers are guaranteed on a visible cadence; they may not depend on an unreachable or silently broken condition.
- Earned traits compose by default. Any replacement must be explicit, but the current direction strongly prefers additive grafts.
- Offers must report concrete current-to-next deltas and expose caps.
- A build must become visually and mechanically legible on the body.

### Production and investment law

- Real graphics are not authorized by this packet. The loop must earn them.
- Procedural infinity may not conceal an unproven first sector.
- The opening ninety seconds must be deliberately authored and testable.
- After the authored opening passes, a pressure director may recombine bounded sector atoms indefinitely.
- The target surface is phone-first and touch-readable.

## Rejected Contracts

- v20: autonomous combat with locomotion that did not author violence.
- v21: manual designation, HE/AP choice, aiming, and player-triggered fire.
- v22: two-tread-only combat that lasted roughly thirty seconds, exposed no upgrade choice, and was not actually endless.
- Circular open-field survivor-like kiting.
- Automatic weapons that can shoot through any angle or obstruction.
- Ambient machine-gun hull drain.
- Stat-only upgrade sludge.
- A scripted "victory" after the first capture.
- Spending on real graphics before duration, cadence, and build mutation are proven.

## Compatible Thunder Evidence

Use these cards and records as ingredients, not as permission to copy their incompatible inputs:

- `survivorlike_offer_loop`: repeated pressure earns build-changing offers while motion remains consequential.
- `additive_trait_mutation_stack`: orthogonal flags, levels, caps, concrete deltas, and shared resolution paths.
- `pressure_arena_event_cloud`: a pressure-defined arena recombines threads and reactions from state.
- `deck_pressure_run_manager`: bounded decks, cooldowns, thresholds, and resource axes schedule escalation.
- `vehicle_body_as_dungeon`: movement, repair, external pressure, and transformation overlap in one machine.
- `highway_vehicle_survivorlike`: forward motion, generated terrain, lane commitment, and resource temptation; reject endless-road repetition and cockpit button creep.
- `pursuer_rearm_gap`: committed contact attacks require approach, retreat, and readable rearming.
- `branching_airborne_enemy_family`: breaking one threat may reveal a new short-term threat while retaining a shared damage path.
- `visible_kill_chain_multiplier`: a readable chain can express clean momentum; use only if it reinforces ground-taking rather than becoming decorative score.
- Armor Command session lessons: independent weapon rhythms, multi-axis pressure, visible HP cues, wave-clear upgrade gates, branching enemies, centralized kill handling, and additive mutations.
- WWDD Gold Rules: feedback becomes harness; additive unless replacement is explicit; pressure field over number soup; smallest playable slice first.

## Incompatible Thunder Evidence

Do not inherit these parts even when their source cards are useful:

- `tap_hold_missile_command_survivorlike` input semantics.
- Full-surface touch aiming.
- Tap missiles.
- Hold-fire turret streams.
- Fixed-base defense.
- Upgrade offers that appear only after a fragile "every hostile array is empty" check.
- Vehicle HUD zones beyond the two tread rails.

## Required Proposal Shape

Produce three proposals that differ in run structure, not merely loadout.

Each proposal must specify:

- core fantasy;
- movement-to-violence law;
- endless run structure;
- authored first ninety seconds;
- guaranteed upgrade cadence;
- at least six additive graft examples with concrete behavioral deltas;
- enemy families and at least four independent pressure axes;
- how infantry survive, cross, and convert breach into ground;
- how captured ground feeds the machine and war party;
- loss states;
- instrumentation and acceptance tests;
- implementation risk;
- what it inherits and rejects from Thunder evidence.

## Required Synthesis Shape

Recommend one prototype structure and identify which pieces, if any, should be borrowed from the other two.

The recommendation must include:

- a second-by-second opening ninety-second beat sheet;
- the repeatable endless macro-cycle;
- a five-minute upgrade schedule;
- initial graft deck and eligibility rules;
- director escalation axes;
- hard acceptance gates;
- explicit reasons the recommendation is not v20, v21, or v22.

## Minimum Acceptance Gates

A candidate has not earned real graphics until all of these pass:

- A ten-minute automated smoke run has no terminal victory state and produces at least eight sector transitions.
- A human five-minute run receives its first upgrade no later than sixty seconds and at least five total upgrade choices.
- Battle telemetry contains only left tread, right tread, and upgrade-card inputs.
- Every organ shot records a valid arc, unobstructed physical line, and target class.
- Steering changes valid firing opportunities measurably; replaying the same seed with materially different tread input produces materially different kills, infantry survival, or capture timing.
- Infantry survival is necessary for every capture.
- MG fire cannot damage the living hull.
- AP/artillery/satchel damage cannot occur without a readable commitment or warning window.
- At least one first-choice graft changes the immediately following counterattack in a visible way.
- No upgrade silently removes an earned behavior.
- The run may fail from hull loss or war-party collapse, never from an arbitrary timer.
